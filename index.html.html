힌트: 전환어(however, rather, therefore)를 먼저 찾고 방향이 바뀌는 지점을 체크하세요.
선택 0 / 5
1Recently, automation has reshaped the nature of work in fundamental ways.
2This shift, however, is not simply a story of job loss.
3Rather, it represents a redefinition of human labor itself.
4For example, workers who once performed repetitive tasks are now expected to manage and interpret machine outputs.
5Therefore, the key challenge is not eliminating jobs, but transforming the skills workers need to thrive.
